Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Your Trust is Our Commitment

At PJB Technical Solutions, we value the trust you place in us when inviting us to handle your personal devices, home networks, and commercial IT infrastructure. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of confidentiality and security.

Data Collection and Usage

We will not use customer information for any purposes other than what is necessary to perform the requested work. This includes:

  • Contacting you regarding scheduling, diagnostics, or invoicing.
  • Establishing remote connection sessions (only with your explicit permission).
  • Configuring network settings, software updates, or migrating user data as approved by you.

Data Security

We employ strict security measures to protect any credentials, system details, or files we access during diagnostics or data migrations. We do not store client credentials or sensitive data on our personal systems longer than required to complete the specific service ticket.

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your contact info, email address, or system configurations with third-party advertisers, marketers, or any external entities. Your information remains strictly between you and your Tech Concierge.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us directly at info@pjbtechsolutions.com.
