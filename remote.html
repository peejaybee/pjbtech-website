Connection Guide

How To Connect

Follow these three simple steps to establish a secure remote connection with your support technician.

01

Download & Save

Click the download button above to save the remote support client file onto your computer (typically saved to your Downloads folder).

02

Run the Client

Windows: Double-click to run (select 'Run' or 'Yes' if prompted). macOS: Open the DMG file, drag the application to Applications, and launch it.

03

Share Credentials

Read the unique ID and Password displayed on the screen to your support concierge to initiate your secure remote session.

Your Session is Fully Secure: The support client uses end-to-end encryption to protect all transmission. Your support concierge can only access your computer while the application is active and open. Once you close the app, all remote access is completely terminated and cannot be re-established without your permission.
