Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our service areas, hardware support capabilities, and policies.

Do you support Apple/Mac devices?

Yes, absolutely! We support both macOS and Windows platforms, as well as iOS, iPadOS, and Android mobile operating systems.

Where do you perform on-site visits?

We provide on-site technical concierge visits within a 20-mile travel radius of Johnsburg, which includes Johnsburg, McHenry, Crystal Lake, Woodstock, Lake Villa, and surrounding communities. For clients outside our physical service radius, we offer comprehensive remote support options.

What constitutes a "flat-fee" service vs. "hourly"?

Flat-fee services (like virus removal or OS tune-ups) cover the entire completed service regardless of how many hours it takes us. Hourly services cover diagnostics and generalized troubleshooting. We always outline estimates before beginning any work.

How does remote helpdesk support work?

With your permission, you download a secure, lightweight remote client utility that provides us temporary access to control your system. You can view the entire process on your screen, and the connection is fully closed once the ticket is complete.
