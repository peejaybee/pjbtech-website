Welcome to **PJB Technology Solutions**. By engaging us for residential tech concierge, computer diagnostic, Wi-Fi configuration, or commercial IT support services, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions.

1. Payment Terms & Invoicing

We maintain simple, transparent pricing structures. Depending on the service category:

  • Hourly Services: Payment for all hourly technical services (both residential and commercial) will be due immediately upon completion of the work on the day of service.
  • Flat-Fee Services: Payment is due upon successful completion of the defined flat-fee service (e.g. Virus Removal or OS Tune-Up).

2. Equipment & Hardware Procurement

As detailed on our Services catalog, listed labor rates assume that you, the customer, provide all necessary equipment, licenses, and cables. If you request that we recommend, source, select, or buy hardware/software on your behalf:

PJB Technology Solutions is not liable for data loss or hardware failure that occurs during normal troubleshooting actions.
